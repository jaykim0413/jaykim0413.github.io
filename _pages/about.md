Hi, I'm Jay — an Electrical Engineering student at the University of
Illinois at Urbana-Champaign, where I'm also pursuing minors in
**Psychology** and **Semiconductor Engineering**. I like building things
that sit right at the boundary between hardware and software, where a bit
of careful circuit design and a bit of code have to meet in the middle.

My primary interests are **chips and embedded systems** — I'm drawn to
problems where I can reason about a system end to end, from the silicon
and the signal, up through the firmware, to the behavior you actually
see. Secondarily, I enjoy working in **robotics and machine learning**,
which give me larger systems to point that low-level thinking at. My minor
in psychology also quietly shapes how I approach design and problem-solving.

### What I've been working on

- **Pressure Piano (ECE 145)** — a pressure-sensitive electronic piano
  with analog tone generation and pressure-based volume control. Details
  are in my [portfolio](/portfolio/).
- **Dental panoramic X-ray segmentation** — a deep-learning approach
  (U-Net + CBAM) for segmenting the maxillary sinus and mandibular
  canal, published at the BioImage Computing Workshop, ECCV 2024. You
  can read more on my [publications](/publications/) page.
